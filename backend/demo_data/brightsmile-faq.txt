BrightSmile Dental - Frequently asked questions

Q: Where is the clinic?
A: We are at 48 Larkspur Avenue, Suite 200, Millbrook.

Q: How do I book an appointment?
A: Book online at brightsmile.example.com, call reception on (555) 555-0147, or email hello@brightsmile.example.com.

Q: What should I bring to my first visit?
A: Photo ID, your insurance card and a list of any medicines you take. Please arrive 15 minutes early to fill in a medical history form.

Q: Do you see children?
A: Yes, from age 2. A children's check-up for under-12s costs $60.

Q: How much is a check-up and clean?
A: A check-up and clean costs $95. We recommend one every 6 months.

Q: Can I cancel or move my appointment?
A: Yes, with at least 24 hours' notice. Later cancellations and missed appointments are charged $40.

Q: Do you accept my insurance?
A: We are in network with Northwind Health, Bluepeak Dental Plans, Harbor Mutual and Evergreen Care. With other insurers you pay in full and claim back with our itemised receipt.

Q: I have toothache at the weekend. What should I do?
A: Call our emergency line on (555) 555-0199. An on-call dentist answers from 8:00 am to 8:00 pm on weekends and public holidays.

Q: Do you offer payment plans?
A: Yes. Treatments over $1,000 can be spread over up to 12 months at 0% interest after a $200 deposit.

Q: How long does teeth whitening take?
A: In-office whitening takes one 90-minute visit and costs $350.
